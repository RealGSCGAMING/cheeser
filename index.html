About Us

Our Story

"Welcome to Cheeser Inc, the multimillion dollar mining company on the Moon!"

One day our founder, Mr. Cheeser, purchased a one-way Uber trip to the Moon, hoping to strike it rich. When he arrived, he realized that the Moon was actually made of cheese! As such, Cheeser Inc was formed to mine the cheese and return it to Earth.

Our Mission

Cheeser Inc is dedicated to designing and producing the best products in the world. Yes, the entire world. There is no company that makes better products than us.
